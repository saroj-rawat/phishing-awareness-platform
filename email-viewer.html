PHISHING ATTACK EXAMPLES

Review the email examples below and learn how attackers use phishing techniques to trick users into revealing sensitive information.



🟥 Example 1 - Phishing Email

This email contains several warning signs commonly found in phishing attacks.

From: security-update@verification-portal-secure.com (Spoofed External Domain)
Subject: Security Alert: Verify Your Account.

Hello User,

We detected unusual activity on your account.

To prevent unauthorized access, please verify your account immediately using the secure link below.

Verification Link:

http://secure-login-account.com/verify.php

Failure to complete this verification within 24 hours will result in automatic account suspension.

Why This Email Is Suspicious

Unknown Sender Domain
The sender's email address uses an unfamiliar domain instead of the organization's official email domain.

Urgent Language
The email pressures the user to act immediately without giving time to verify the request.

Threat of Account Suspension
Creating fear is a common phishing technique used to encourage quick decisions.

Suspicious Verification Link
Unexpected verification links may redirect users to fake login pages designed to steal usernames and passwords.



🟩 Example 2 - Legitimate Email

Compare the phishing email above with this legitimate security notification.

From: security@company.com
Subject: Security Notice: New Sign-in Detected

Hello User,

We detected a sign-in to your account from a new device.

If this was you, no further action is required.

If you do not recognize this activity, please visit our official website below.

Official Website

https://company.com/account/security

Thank you,
Security Team

Why This Email Looks Safe

Official Sender
The sender uses the organization's official email domain instead of an unfamiliar or misleading address.

No Urgent Language
The email informs the user about account activity without creating panic or forcing immediate action.

No Request for Passwords
Legitimate organizations do not ask users to share passwords or sensitive information through email.

Safe Instructions
Instead of asking users to click an unexpected verification link, the email directs them to the company's official website, where they can safely review their account activity.



🟧 Example 3 - More Convincing Phishing Email

This email looks more realistic than the first one. Try to spot the warning signs before reading the explanation.

From: security@company-support.com (Fake Domain That Looks Real)
Subject: Security Notice: New Sign-in Detected

Hello User,

We detected a sign-in to your account from a new device.

If this was you, no action is required.

If you do not recognize this activity, please review your account below.

Review Activity

https://company-security-login.com/verify.php

Please complete verification within 12 hours to avoid temporary account suspension.

Why This Email Is Suspicious

Look-alike Sender Domain
The sender uses company-support.com instead of the official company domain.

Unnecessary Verification
Although the email says no action is required, it later asks the user to verify their identity.

Creates Urgency
The 12-hour deadline creates pressure to act quickly.

Suspicious Website Link
The email includes a website link that asks users to verify their account immediately. Always check whether the website address belongs to the official organization before visiting it.
