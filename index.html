🎣 What is Phishing?

Imagine receiving an email from your bank or university asking you to verify your account immediately. The email looks genuine, uses official logos, and even includes a professional-looking website link. Without realizing it, you enter your login details into a fake website created by an attacker.

This type of cyber attack is known as phishing. It is a technique used by cybercriminals to impersonate trusted individuals or organizations in order to steal sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, One-Time Passwords (OTPs), banking details, and other personal information.

Unlike many cyber attacks that target computer systems, phishing attacks target people by manipulating emotions such as fear, urgency, trust, and curiosity. This technique is known as social engineering.

📌 Project Scope

Phishing attacks can occur through emails, text messages (smishing), phone calls (vishing), fake websites, and social media. However, this project focuses specifically on email phishing awareness.

The website demonstrates realistic phishing scenarios through email examples, a URL Risk Analyzer, security tips, and a phishing awareness quiz. All examples are created strictly for educational purposes and do not collect any personal information.

⚠️ Why is Phishing Dangerous?

A successful phishing attack can lead to serious consequences for both individuals and organizations.

  • 🔒 Identity theft
  • 💳 Financial fraud and monetary loss
  • 🔑 Stolen usernames, passwords, and OTPs
  • 💻 Malware or ransomware infections
  • 📂 Unauthorized access to personal or business accounts
  • 📄 Loss of confidential information

🤝 Understanding Social Engineering

Most phishing attacks succeed because they manipulate human behaviour rather than computer systems. Attackers create a false sense of urgency, fear, curiosity, or trust to convince users to click malicious links or share confidential information.

Throughout this project, you will learn how to recognize these psychological tricks before they become successful phishing attacks.
