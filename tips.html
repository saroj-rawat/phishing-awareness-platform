🛡️ Security Tips & Best Practices

Learning to recognize phishing emails is only the first step. Following simple security practices can greatly reduce the risk of becoming a victim of phishing attacks and help protect your personal and organizational information.

📧 Verify the Sender

Always check the sender's email address before trusting a message. Attackers often use email addresses that look similar to legitimate organizations but contain small spelling changes or extra words.

🔗 Check Website Links Carefully

Before clicking any link, hover over it to view the full website address. Make sure the domain belongs to the official organization and avoid clicking shortened or suspicious links.

🔒 Protect Personal Information

Legitimate organizations rarely ask for passwords, OTPs, or banking information through email. Never share sensitive information unless you have verified the request through an official channel.

🖇️ Be Careful with Attachments

Do not open unexpected email attachments, especially files ending in .exe, .zip, or unknown document formats. These files may contain malicious software.

🛡️ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Using Multi-Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second verification step in addition to your password.

💻 Keep Your Software Updated

Install operating system and application updates regularly. Security updates fix vulnerabilities that attackers may try to exploit.

🚩 Report Suspicious Emails

If you receive a suspicious email, report it to your organization's IT or security team instead of replying or clicking any links.


✅ Quick Safety Checklist

  • Verify the sender's email address.
  • Inspect website links before clicking.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Keep software and antivirus updated.
  • Report suspicious emails immediately.

Stay Alert, Stay Secure

Cybersecurity begins with awareness. By recognizing phishing attempts and following safe online practices, users can significantly reduce the risk of identity theft, financial fraud, and unauthorized access to sensitive information.
